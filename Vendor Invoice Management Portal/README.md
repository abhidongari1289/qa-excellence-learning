## 🎯 Use Case: Vendor Invoice Management Portal

### Client Requirements:
1. Vendors can register and log in
2. Vendors can submit invoices against purchase orders
3. AP team can view, approve, or reject invoices
4. Approved invoices forwarded to payment processing
5. Email notifications on status changes
6. Monthly invoice activity reports
7. Only authorized users may access
